So Who Is This Johnnie Brown?

Johnnie Brown’s is a Delray Beach institution.

For over 10 years, Johnnie Brown’s has been the place locals come to for casual dining, gathering with friends for drinks, and the best live music in South Florida. Serving up classic favorites like burgers, wings, ribs, and locally caught seafood like the Swordfish Sliders, the revamped menu has something for everyone. Johnnie Brown’s bartenders are serving up ice cold cocktails, beers and frozen drinks like the Foreigner with Rose, fresh watermelon and Vodka.

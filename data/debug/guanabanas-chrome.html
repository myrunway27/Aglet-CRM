Our Story

Opened by Jupiter surfers as a sandwich shop in 2004, Guanabanas has grown to become an institution in northern Palm Beach County for three reasons: The lush, tropical setting; great cuisine and cocktails; and live music from both national acts and the hottest names in South Florida.

About Us

Guanabanas is a 100 percent open-air restaurant and bar with woven tiki huts and banyan trees towering overhead, and hand-chiseled coquina stone pathways underfoot. The team maintains a laid-back, island atmosphere where everyone is welcome.

Giving Back

The success of Guanabanas has been due to the fantastic support of our local community. This success has given us the opportunity to "Give Back" to our community to such great organizations such as Mia's Miracles, Place of Hope, R3 Foundation Florida, Freedom 4 Miler, South Florida Soup Kitchen and Food Assistance, Shifting Gears United, Fort Freedom, Pipers Angels Foundation, Chasin A Dream Foundation, Jupiter Narrows Conservancy, Ocean Conservancy, Surfrider Foundation, Stoked On Life Foundation, RiseUp Kids International, House of Hope in Martin County, Humane Society of the Treasure Coast and countless of other local groups and foundations.

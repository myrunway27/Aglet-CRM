FAQ

Questions? We've Got Answers.

A merchant cash advance (MCA) is not a loan — it's a purchase of a portion of your business's future receivables at a discount. You receive a lump sum of working capital today, and we collect an agreed-upon amount from your future revenue through fixed daily or weekly remittances until the purchased amount is satisfied.

Most complete applications receive a decision the same business day. Once you accept your offer and final verification is complete, funds are typically wired within 24 hours — many clients see money in their account the next business morning.

Yes. Approval is based primarily on your business's revenue and bank activity, not your personal credit score. Business owners with past bankruptcies, tax liens, or low FICO scores are approved regularly — as long as the business itself is generating consistent revenue.

Just our one-page application and your business's last 3–4 months of bank statements. For larger funding amounts we may request a few additional items, but there are no tax returns, business plans, or financial projections required to get an offer.

Remittances are collected automatically via small, fixed daily or weekly ACH debits from your business bank account — no checks to write and no due dates to remember. The schedule is sized to your cash flow before you sign, and many programs offer early payoff discounts.
